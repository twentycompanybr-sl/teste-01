Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Rafa Boni Aesthetics (“we”, “us”), founded by Rafaela Bonifácio, offering aesthetic treatments in London, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at rafaboni_aesthetics@outlook.com or on WhatsApp at +44 7588 723507.

What we collect

When you send a consultation request or contact us, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, preferred location, treatment of interest, preferred date and any additional message.

Please do not send medical information or photographs through the website form. Any health information needed for your treatment is collected separately during your consultation.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a consultation (lawful basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract).
  • To keep a record of our communication with you (lawful basis: our legitimate interest in managing enquiries).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for marketing without your separate consent.

Who processes it

Form submissions are stored by our website hosting provider (Netlify) on our behalf. If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram or by email, those services process your messages under their own privacy policies. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to respond and manage your request — normally no longer than 12 months if you do not become a client — unless a longer period is required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We store your language preference in your browser so the site opens in the language you chose. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at rafaboni_aesthetics@outlook.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
