FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know before booking. For anything else, we are happy to help on WhatsApp.

01Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Every treatment starts with a consultation, where we talk about your goals, review your medical history and check whether a treatment is suitable for you. If it isn’t the right option for you, we will tell you honestly.

02Do the treatments hurt?

Most clients find treatments very manageable. The sensation varies by treatment and from person to person. We explain what to expect beforehand and discuss comfort measures, such as topical numbing where appropriate.

03How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment, your skin and your goals. Some treatments are one-off, while others work best as a course. You will receive a personalised plan at your consultation.

04Is there any downtime?

It varies. After treatments such as a hydrofacial most people return to their routine straight away, while microneedling or peels may leave the skin red or sensitive for a few days. We go through what to expect for your specific treatment.

05When will I see results?

Some treatments give an immediate glow, while others, such as collagen-stimulating treatments, develop gradually over several weeks. Results vary from person to person and are never guaranteed.

06Who are the treatments suitable for?

Treatments are available to clients aged 18 and over, following a suitability assessment. Some treatments are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with certain health conditions or medications, so please let us know about these at your consultation.

07Do you offer prescription-only treatments?

Prescription-only medicines are never offered without an individual, face-to-face assessment by an appropriately qualified prescriber, who decides whether any treatment is appropriate for you.

08How do I look after my skin after treatment?

You will receive personalised before- and aftercare advice for your treatment. Daily sun protection is one of the most important steps to protect your skin and your results.

09Where are you located?

We see clients in Canada Water and Mayfair, London. Get in touch on WhatsApp or by email for location details.

10How do I book and pay?

Send a consultation request through the website or message us on WhatsApp; your appointment is only booked once it is confirmed with you. We accept card, Apple Pay, PayPal, Revolut Pay, Klarna and Clearpay.
