About

Meet Rafaela Bonifácio

I am Rafaela Bonifácio, founder of Rafa Boni Aesthetics. With academic backgrounds in Aesthetics and Food Engineering and three years of experience in the beauty and advanced aesthetics industry, I am passionate about skin health and enhancing each person’s natural beauty.

My approach combines professional knowledge, modern techniques and personalised care, always focusing on natural-looking, harmonious results that help my clients feel confident and comfortable in their own skin.

Every client is unique, which is why I believe in creating treatments tailored to individual needs, goals and expectations.
