Body & Specialised Treatments

Tattoo & Pigmentation Removal

Laser treatment breaks tattoo ink and unwanted pigment into tiny particles that the body can gradually clear. The number of sessions depends on the colours, depth and age of the tattoo or pigmentation.

Request a consultation Ask on WhatsApp

May help with

  • Unwanted tattoos
  • Fading before a cover-up
  • Old cosmetic or brow tattoos
  • Pigmentation and sun spots

How it works

  1. 01

    Your consultation

    We talk about your goals and medical history, assess suitability and agree a plan together, including what is realistic for you.

  2. 02

    On the day

    Your treatment takes place in a calm, private setting, with your comfort as a priority. We explain every step as we go.

  3. 03

    Aftercare

    The area may be red, swollen or blistered for a few days and must be kept clean and protected from the sun. Sessions are spaced several weeks apart to allow the skin to heal.

Treatments are subject to a consultation and suitability assessment. Individual results vary.
