Winter skincare in London: how to protect your skin barrier

Cold wind, central heating and grey days can leave skin dry and dull. Simple, practical steps to keep your skin comfortable through a London winter.

A London winter is hard on the skin. Cold, windy days outside and dry central heating inside can leave it tight, flaky, sensitive or dull — even if your skin behaves perfectly the rest of the year. The good news: a few simple adjustments make a real difference.

Why skin struggles in winter

Your skin barrier is the outer layer that keeps moisture in and irritants out. Low humidity, temperature changes and hot showers all make it easier for water to escape, so the barrier becomes weaker and the skin feels drier and more reactive.

1. Cleanse gently

Swap foaming or stripping cleansers for a gentle cream, milk or gel cleanser, and use lukewarm rather than hot water. If your skin feels tight after cleansing, your cleanser is probably too strong.

2. Layer hydration, then seal it in

Hydrating ingredients such as hyaluronic acid or glycerin help draw water into the skin; a moisturiser with ceramides or fatty acids then helps keep it there. In winter, many people benefit from a slightly richer moisturiser than in summer.

3. Don’t skip sunscreen

UV rays are still present on cloudy days, and they contribute to pigmentation and visible ageing. A broad-spectrum SPF every morning remains one of the most effective habits for healthy-looking skin — all year round.

4. Go easy on exfoliation

Exfoliation can help with dullness, but over-exfoliating a compromised barrier often leads to redness and sensitivity. In winter, one or two gentle sessions a week is usually plenty — and pause strong actives if your skin starts to sting.

5. Think about your environment

A humidifier at home, drinking enough water and protecting your skin from wind with a scarf all help. Small, consistent habits matter more than any single product.

When a professional treatment can help

Winter is also a popular time for treatments focused on hydration and skin quality. A hydrofacial can deep-cleanse and rehydrate without downtime, while skin boosters or Profhilo® are designed to improve hydration from within. The right option depends on your skin — and some treatments are better suited to the colder months, when sun exposure is lower.

If your skin is feeling the effects of the season, a consultation is a good place to start: we will look at your routine, your skin and your goals, and suggest what makes sense for you.
