What is PDRN? A simple guide to polynucleotide skin treatments

PDRN and polynucleotides are among the most talked-about ingredients in aesthetics. Here is what they are, how they are used and what to expect.

If you follow skincare on social media, you have probably heard of PDRN — sometimes called “salmon DNA” treatments. It is one of the most talked-about ingredients in aesthetics right now, so it is worth understanding what it is, and what it is not.

What does PDRN stand for?

PDRN means polydeoxyribonucleotide: fragments of DNA, usually purified from salmon or trout, that are highly compatible with human tissue. A closely related family of ingredients is known as polynucleotides. Both have been used in medicine and dermatology for some years, particularly in the context of tissue repair.

How is it used in aesthetics?

In aesthetic treatments, PDRN is mainly used in two ways:

  • With microneedling, where tiny, controlled micro-channels help the product reach the skin while also stimulating its natural renewal process.
  • As an injectable skin booster, placed in small amounts across the treatment area.

In both cases, the aim is to support skin repair and quality — rather than to add volume or change the shape of the face.

Who might consider it?

PDRN-based treatments are often considered by people who want to improve the appearance of:

  • skin texture and post-acne marks;
  • dull, tired-looking or dehydrated skin;
  • fine lines and overall skin quality.

It is not suitable for everyone. Allergies (including to fish), pregnancy, active skin infections and some medical conditions may mean another option is more appropriate — which is exactly why a consultation comes first.

What to expect

After microneedling with PDRN, redness similar to mild sunburn is common for one to three days. After injectable treatments, small bumps at the injection points usually settle within a day or two. Most people see a gradual improvement in skin quality over the following weeks, and a course of sessions is often recommended. As with any treatment, results vary from person to person.

The bottom line

PDRN is a promising ingredient for skin quality, but it works best as part of a plan built around your skin. At Rafa Boni Aesthetics, every treatment starts with a consultation where we look at your goals, your history and whether PDRN — or something else — is the right choice for you.
